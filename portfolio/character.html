Character Design

For my character design work, I usually work with traditionally with Copic Markers and Micron Fineliners.

My Process

Thumbnail Sketches

I usually start out by creating a variety of rough thumbnail sketches for the general vibe, shape language, and silhouette. These are the thumbnail sketches for Max, the protagonist of my webcomic, Max Brimstone. My goal was to create a character that looked mischevious, rumpled, and a bit dorky. I was inspired by early 2000's grunge fashion.

Rough Color

Next I will create a couple of rough color sketches to determine the color pallette. For Max, I knew I wanted to incorporate green, to make him distinct from other imp characters in popular culture. I ended up creating a couple of different pallettes, some focusing more on a greenish-yellow pallette, some focusing more on contrasting green with warm browns, and some focusing on using green and yellow to contrast warm grays.

Iteration

I will then iterate and create multiple versions of the design.

Final Product

Finally I will take what I learned from the previous stages, and use it to create a finalized design.
